---

### Quick Directory Layout:
- `raw/`: Read-only source documents (drop new PDFs, research notes, and web-clips here).
- `wiki/`: Compounding, interconnected knowledge layers maintained by the AI agent.
- `templates/`: Markdown templates for concepts, entities, methods, and sources.
- `scripts/`: System health check (`python scripts/wiki_lint.py`).
- `tests/`: Automated acceptance tests (`python -m unittest tests/test_wiki.py`).
